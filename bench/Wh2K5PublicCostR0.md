leak/fake-stack checks. Independent polynomial arithmetic derives selected
K5 rows and payloads, reconstructs all control payloads from native basis
probes, and verifies each decoder's first-success rank. Backend fixtures
must agree. Exercise all 108 WORK cases, the complete roster, claim binding,

queue, allocation or thread. The original reference corpus supplies the
header even if working fixtures are corrupted. Publication is single-pass;

reconstructed data. The controller preserves the captured prefix on output
caps/timeouts and reaps its child. Partial records, missing output, nonzero

failure retention, identity/descriptor substitution, complete manifests, and
adversarial producer/dependency checks. Exact full replay plus a separately
written whole-raw/statistical/provenance audit must finish before advancing
